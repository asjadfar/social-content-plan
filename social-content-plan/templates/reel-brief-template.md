# Reel Brief Template — 7pm slot, one per day

> One brief per reel. One product or bundle only (or the sale itself for a sale reel).
> The owner renders the clips in Flow from this brief and sends the reel back.
> Check the reel frame by frame before scheduling — do not post a flawed reel.

## Reel

- **Day / slot:** [Day, DD MMM — 7:00pm MYT (4pm PKT)]
- **Product / bundle:** [one only — exact name, size and verified live price]
- **Reel type:** [hero product / His & Her Bundle / sale reel / festive-gift reel]
- **Verified live price:** [RM ___ (was RM ___) — checked DD MMM]

## Build

- **Length:** 15 seconds (real builds ranged 10–18 seconds; the end card is always exactly 2 seconds)
- **Ratio:** 9:16 portrait, 720×1280
- **Music:** [yes — upbeat / festive / luxury, matching the reel type]
- **Scenes:** [for example product photo 5s → pair/studio photo 5s → offer slide 3s → end card 2s]
- **Hook (first 5 seconds):** [the unskippable opening — product, offer or question, on screen in the first 5 seconds]
- **End card (last 2 seconds, exactly):** `SHOP NOW AT FRAGRANCEEMPIRE.MY` — nothing after it
- **Cover image (Instagram):** [one still frame, portrait 4:5, product clearly visible]

## Caption

Write it the same way as the caption template (FB + IG versions), with the verified price and the offer block.

- **Caption (FB):** [exact text — direct shop link, ≤ 1,024 characters]
