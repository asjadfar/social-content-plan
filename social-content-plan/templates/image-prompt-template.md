# Image-Prompt Template — one photographic prompt per article post

> Send each prompt to the owner as a standalone copyable message.
> The owner renders it in ChatGPT and sends the image back.
> Never write a tip-card prompt. Never skip the text-overlay rule.

## The fixed pattern (copy this structure every time)

```
Photographic luxury perfume image, [scene: product, setting, light, mood],
premium advertising photography. Large bold metallic gold 3D text at the top
reads '[HEADLINE IN CAPITALS]'. Large bold metallic gold 3D text at the
bottom reads 'CHOOSE LUXURY', with small elegant white text below it reading
'fragranceempire.my'. No other text.
```

Rules for every prompt:

1. **Photographic.** It must describe a real photo scene — product, setting, light, mood. No tip-card, no graphic, no text-heavy design.
2. **Text-overlay rule (all three, every time):**
   - Headline in large bold metallic gold 3D text at the TOP — the post's headline, in capitals, exactly the same words as the caption's first line.
   - `CHOOSE LUXURY` in large bold metallic gold 3D text at the BOTTOM.
   - `fragranceempire.my` in small elegant white text at the VERY BOTTOM.
   - End with: `No other text.`
3. **Product matches the caption.** The bottle in the prompt is the product in the caption. A bundle post shows the bundle pair. Never a bottle showing a different variant (for example "200ml" text on a 100 ML listing).
4. **One prompt per post.** Do not combine two posts into one prompt.

- [ ] Headline at the top, in large bold metallic gold 3D text
- [ ] `CHOOSE LUXURY` at the bottom, in large bold metallic gold 3D text
- [ ] `fragranceempire.my` at the very bottom, small elegant white text
- [ ] No other text anywhere in the image
- [ ] Photographic, not a tip-card or graphic
- [ ] Product matches the caption (right bottle, right size, right variant)

If anything fails, say so plainly and ask for a re-render — do not post a wrong image.
